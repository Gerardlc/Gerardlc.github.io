LeoBaby: foreground service demonstration

Package com.leobaby, version 0.1.1 (2). These screen recordings show the signed release on two Android emulators connected through the emulator Wi-Fi network. The camera image is the emulator's virtual camera; no people or personal recordings appear.

Camera and microphone

The user chooses Camera and starts it, grants camera, microphone and notification permissions, opens pairing, compares both device fingerprints and confirms the connection. Camera capture runs with the app visible and the display kept on. The user stops streaming from the app.

Monitor: media playback

The user connects to the authorized Camera and grants notification permission. The Monitor shows live video and active audio, continues its playback service when the user returns to Android Home, and displays an ongoing notification. Pressing Stop in that notification stops the Monitor service.

Recorded on 9 October 2026. Screen recordings have no soundtrack. These are short service demonstrations, not physical-device or battery endurance tests.
